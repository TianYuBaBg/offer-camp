Java in English · for a complete beginner英文 Java · 写给完全零基础

One small page. One idea.一次只读一页，一次只学一个概念。

I know nothing about Java. I want to learn it in English, not in Chinese. So every page gives one idea, five new words, and three questions I answer out loud.我不懂 Java，而且我想用英文学，不是用中文背。所以每一页只讲一个概念、给五个新词、三道要出声回答的题。

Short answer先给答案This site teaches Java in English, one idea per page: seven short lessons, then the HashMap interview question.这个站用英文教 Java，一页只讲一个概念：先上七节短课，再读 HashMap 那道面试题。

01The line: one question, many branches主线：从一个问题长出去

Everything starts from one question. Each station below is a place the answer opens a new question. Read down the line, not across the table.所有内容都从一个问题长出来。线上一站就是一处“答案又生出一个新问题”的地方。顺着线往下读，不要横着扫。

  1. 01What is a HashMap, really?HashMap 到底是个什么东西？An array of buckets; each bucket holds a short list.一个桶数组，每个桶挂一条短链。第 1 题 · 已上线
  2. 02How does a key end up in one bucket?一个 key 怎么就跑到某个桶里？hash it, then h & (n - 1).先算 hash，再 h & (n-1) 取下标。第 4 课
  3. 03Two keys, one bucket. Now what?两个 key 撞进同一个桶，怎么办？Stand in a line — a linked list; after 8, a tree.排队——挂成链表；到 8 个就转成红黑树。第 2–3 课
  4. 04The buckets are getting full. Then?桶快满了呢？At 75% the array doubles; each key stays or moves +n.装到 75% 翻倍；每个 key 要么原位、要么 +n。第 5 课
  5. 05Two threads put at the same time?两个线程同时 put 会怎样？Lost data, or a loop in JDK 7. Use ConcurrentHashMap.丢数据，JDK 7 还会成环。换 ConcurrentHashMap。第 7 课
  6. 06So how is it used as a cache at work?那工作中怎么拿它当缓存用？Redis in front of the DB — and three ways the cache bites back.Redis 挡在数据库前面——以及缓存咬你的三种方式。待写
  7. 07WeChat sends the same callback three times. Then?微信同时回调三条，怎么办？Idempotent table, then status CAS, then a lock.幂等表 → 状态 CAS → 再上锁。第 2 题 · 已上线
  8. 08Ten thousand people, one thousand items. No oversell?一万人抢一千件，怎么不超卖？Pre-deduct in Redis, then let the DB be the last check.先在 Redis 预扣，数据库做最后一道闸。待写

Grey stations are not written yet. The line grows as we go — that is the point.灰色的站还没写。线会一直长下去，这本来就是重点。

02Five minutes with your hands动手 5 分钟：把 key 放进桶里

Reading gives you the words. Playing gives you the mechanics. Three levels, about five minutes.读是把话记住，玩是把机制记住。三关，大约 5 分钟。

  • Level 1 — place 5 keys, and watch order and node collide in bucket 15.第一关：放 5 个 key，看 order 和 node 在桶 15 撞成一条链。
  • Level 2 — fill 12 of 16 (that is 75%), then watch the array double and every key either stay or move by 16.第二关：装满 12 个（16 的 75%），看数组翻倍，每个 key 要么原位、要么 +16。
  • Level 3 — the 8th key in one bucket turns the chain into a red-black tree: 8 steps become 3.第三关：同一个桶放第 8 个，链子变成红黑树，8 次比较变成 3 次。
  • Every word on a card can be clicked to hear it read aloud (five sites to choose from).卡片上的每个单词都能点开听真人发音（右上角可选 5 个站）。

04How to read a page怎么读一页

  1. Press 🔊 at the top. Follow the words with your eyes. Twice is enough.点页面顶部的 🔊，眼睛跟着字走。两遍就够。
  2. Switch to 中英 if a sentence is too hard. Read the English first, then the Chinese.句子太难就切到 中英：先读英文，再看中文。
  3. Click ▶ hear it on five new words. Say each one out loud three times.在新词里点 ▶ hear it，听真人发音，每个词大声说三遍。
  4. Answer the three questions at the bottom out loud, not in your head.页面底部三道题要出声回答，不要只在心里想。

Twenty minutes is enough. One station a day, and the line keeps growing.二十分钟就够。一天一站，线就一直在长。

05Why English, and why so slow为什么要英文、为什么这么慢

In an interview the words are English: bucket, collision, resize. If I only know the Chinese word, I hear a sound I cannot follow.面试里蹦出来的是英文词：bucket、collision、resize。如果我只知道中文说法，听到的就是一串抓不住的声音。

So this site is slow on purpose. One idea per page, five words, one picture.所以这个网站是故意慢的：一页一个概念、五个词、一张图。
