About

Learn Java, in English.

Short sentences, real pronunciation, no Chinese in the learning path.

English firstno tracking

01Why this site is in English

I am learning Java, and I am learning English at the same time. If I read Java in Chinese, I only learn the idea. If I read it in English, I learn the idea and the words I will hear in an interview.

So every page here is in English, with short sentences and no idioms. Every technical word has a real pronunciation link.

02How to use it

  • Listen first. Press “Listen to this page” and follow with your eyes.
  • Say the new words. Five words a page. YouGlish gives real people saying them.
  • Answer the checks out loud. If you cannot say it, read the page again.
  • Come back tomorrow. Yesterday's words, then one new page.

03What is on it

  • Basics — seven short lessons: array, linked list, tree, hash, Big O, HashMap methods, threads.
  • Questions — one page per interview question, starting with HashMap.
  • Words — every word in one list, with pronunciation.

The site is plain HTML with no framework and no tracking. It is published from GitHub by Cloudflare Pages.
