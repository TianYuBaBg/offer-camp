Follow-up 04追问 04

It renews a lease, not “the lock forever”.它续的是“租约”，不是“锁永久有效”。

Default lease 30s, renewed every 10s while the holding thread is alive. Kill the process and the lease simply expires.默认 30 秒租约、持锁线程活着就每 10 秒续一次；进程一挂就不再续，30 秒后自动释放。

Short answer先给答案Redisson's watchdog pushes the key's TTL back to 30s every 10s (1/3 of the lease) while the lock-holding thread is alive. It exists so a slow business operation does not lose the lock mid-way — it is not a promise that the lock never expires.Redisson 的 watchdog 在持锁线程活着时每 10 秒把 key 的过期时间重置回 30 秒（租约的 1/3）。它的意义是让慢业务不在中途丢锁，而不是承诺锁永不过期。

01Lease, not ownership租约，不是所有权

Term概念Meaning含义
lockLeaseTimehow long the key lives if nobody renews没人续的话 key 活多久
watchdog period续期周期leaseTime / 3, i.e. every 10sleaseTime 的 1/3，即每 10 秒
process dies进程挂了no renewal → key expires → lock released不再续 → key 过期 → 锁释放

This is the whole point: a crashed holder must not block the world forever.核心就在这里：持锁方崩了，绝不能把所有人锁死。

02The three settings people confuse三个容易搞混的设置

  • lock.lock() — no lease argument, watchdog enabled, renews forever while alive.lock.lock()——不传租期，watchdog 生效，活着就一直续。
  • lock.lock(10, TimeUnit.SECONDS) — fixed lease, watchdog off, unlock after 10s even if you are still working.lock.lock(10, SECONDS)——固定租期，watchdog 关闭，10 秒后即使你还在干活也会解锁。
  • tryLock(wait, lease, unit) — you choose both; the smaller the lease, the more often renewal must succeed.tryLock(wait, lease, unit)——等待和租期都自己定；租期越短，续期越不能失败。

Long jobs plus a fixed lease is the classic bug: the lock releases while the thread is still writing.长任务 + 固定租期是经典事故：线程还在写，锁已经没了。

03What it still cannot save you from它救不了什么

  • A stop-the-world GC pause longer than the lease: the key expires, someone else takes the lock, then your thread wakes up and keeps writing.超过租期的 Full GC 停顿：key 过期、别人拿到锁，而你的线程醒来继续写。
  • Network partition where renewal packets are lost but the thread is alive.网络分区：续期包丢了，线程却还活着。
  • Main-replica switchover: the new master never received the lock write.主从切换：新主从来没收到那次加锁写入。

That is why Redisson locks are a speed bump, not a correctness guarantee. The database's unique index and conditional update are still the last line.所以 Redisson 锁只是减速带，不是正确性保证。兜底的还是数据库唯一索引和条件更新。
