Why spread the high bits into the low bits.为什么要把 hash 的高位异或下来。

Only the low bits pick the bucket, so the top 16 bits are wasted — unless you fold them in.定位只用低位，高 16 位本来纯浪费——除非把它们折进低位。

Short answer先给答案Because bucket index = hash & (n-1) only looks at the low bits. Most hashCodes (string, integer) differ mostly in the high bits, so folding h ^ (h >>> 16) brings that entropy down where it is used.因为桶下标 = hash & (n-1)，只看低位。而多数 hashCode（字符串、整数）的差异主要在高位，所以用 h ^ (h >>> 16) 把高位的熵搬到低位来用。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01A concrete case一个具体例子

// 两个 key 只在高位不同
h1 = 0x1234_0001
h2 = 0xabcd_0001

h1 & 15 = 1
h2 & 15 = 1        // 同一个桶

h1 ^ (h1>>>16) = 0x1234_1235  -> &15 = 5
h2 ^ (h2>>>16) = 0xabcd_abcd  -> &15 = 13   // 分开了

One xor, one shift — and two keys that used to collide are now in different buckets.一次异或、一次移位——原本打架的两个 key 分开了。

02Why not a stronger hash为什么不做更强的 hash

A full rehash (like Murmur) costs more than it saves; one xor-shift is enough for “decent but not hostile” keys. Truly hostile keys need a tree, which is exactly the next follow-up.完整重哈希（如 Murmur）代价高于收益；一次异或移位对“正常但不友善”的 key 已经够。真正恶意构造的 key，靠的是树化，也就是下一条。
