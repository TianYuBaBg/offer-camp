Why the capacity has to be a power of two.为什么容量必须是 2 的幂。

The answer is one line of bit math — and it is also why resize can avoid rehashing.答案是一行位运算——也正是扩容时能避免重算 hash 的原因。

Short answer先给答案If n is a power of two, (n-1) is all ones in binary, so h & (n-1) keeps the low bits and equals h % n — but much faster. If n is not a power of two, some low-bit patterns can never be produced and whole buckets stay empty.容量是 2 的幂时，n-1 的二进制全是 1，所以 h & (n-1) 等于 h % n，但快得多。如果不是 2 的幂，某些低位组合永远取不到，一批桶会空着，浪费空间又加剧冲突。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01Why not modulo为什么不用取模

Division is slow at the CPU level; a bitwise AND is one cycle. With 16 buckets that is the difference between a fast put and a slower one on every single insert.除法在 CPU 里是慢指令，按位与只要一个周期。16 个桶的情况下，这个差别在每次 put 上都存在。

n = 16
h = 0x7f3a
h & (n - 1)   ->  0x0a   (10)
h % n         ->  10        // 结果一样

// n = 15（不是 2 的幂）
h & 14 永远取不到 15，最低位被吃掉

02What it buys during resize扩容时白拿的好处

  • Because capacity doubles, the new mask is the old mask plus one bit.因为容量翻倍，新掩码只比旧掩码多一位。
  • That new bit decides everything: 0 → stay, 1 → move to index + oldCap.这一位决定一切：是 0 就留在原位，是 1 就挪到“原位 + 旧容量”。
  • So resize never recomputes hashCode — it re-splits the chain in two.所以扩容从不重算 hashCode，只是把链撕成两条。
