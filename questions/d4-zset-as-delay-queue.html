Follow-up 01追问 01

A sorted set is a timer with an index.有序集合就是“带索引的定时器”。

score is the due timestamp, so the earliest-due orders sit at the front of the range.score 就是到期时间戳，所以最早到期的单永远排在范围查询的最前面。

Short answer先给答案ZADD puts an order in with score = due time (ms). ZRANGEBYSCORE ... LIMIT pulls the ones already due. Ordering is maintained by the skiplist, so “what is due now” is O(log N) instead of a full scan.ZADD 入队时把 score 设成到期时间戳；ZRANGEBYSCORE 按 score 范围取已到期的。有序性由跳表维护，所以“现在谁到期了”是 O(log N)，不用扫全表。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01The four commands四条命令搞定

-- 入队：到期时间作为 score
ZADD order:delay  ""

-- 取件：score <= now 的，最多 100 条
ZRANGEBYSCORE order:delay 0  LIMIT 0 100

-- 处理成功后移除
ZREM order:delay ""

-- 取消订单时也移除
ZREM order:delay ""

Nothing else is needed: no table scan, no scheduler table, no polling of the orders table.不需要别的：不扫表、不建定时任务表、不轮询订单表。
