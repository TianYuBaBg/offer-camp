Follow-up 05追问 05

A cold cache is the same as no cache, at the worst moment.冷缓存等于没缓存，而且偏偏在最糟的时刻。

Right after a deploy, every node has an empty local cache and Redis may have just been cleared.刚发布完，每个节点本地缓存是空的，Redis 也可能刚清过。

Short answer先给答案Load the top N hot keys at startup (or by a one-off job), and warm the local cache on the first few requests via a slow-start. Without it, the first minute is a full-on avalanche.启动时加载 Top N 热 key（或跑一次性任务），并对前几分钟做慢启动预热本地缓存。不做的话，第一分钟就是一次小型雪崩。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01How to warm怎么预热

  • Startup job: read the top N keys from an offline statistic (or from the DB with a limit).启动任务：按离线统计的 Top N 读回来（或带 limit 查库）。
  • Slow start: let local cache TTL grow from 10s to its normal value over a few minutes.慢启动：本地缓存 TTL 从 10 秒逐步涨到正常值。
  • Canary: release to 5% first, watch DB QPS, then roll out.灰度：先 5%，看数据库 QPS，再全量。

The number that matters is DB QPS right after deploy. Watch it, not the cache hit rate.真正要看的是发布后的数据库 QPS，而不是缓存命中率。
