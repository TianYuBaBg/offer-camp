Why 0.75 and not 0.5 or 1.0.为什么负载因子是 0.75，不是 0.5 也不是 1。

It is the point where space, collision rate and resize cost stop fighting each other.在这个点上，空间、冲突率、扩容成本三者不再互相拉扯。

Short answer先给答案With 0.5 you waste half the array and resize twice as often. With 1.0 the chains get long before you grow, so lookups degrade. 0.75 keeps the expected chain short while still using three quarters of the memory.取 0.5 会浪费一半数组，扩容频率也翻倍；取 1.0 则链先变长、查询先变慢。0.75 既让期望链长很短，又用掉了四分之三的内存。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01Three costs, one knob三个成本，一个旋钮

Load factor负载因子What you pay你付出什么
0.5double memory, double resizes内存翻倍，扩容翻倍
0.75balanced (the default)平衡（默认值）
1.0long chains, slow lookups, spikes at resize链变长、查询变慢、扩容尖峰

Resize is O(n) and copies every entry, so a full table at 1.0 also means the next put pays for a large copy right when the table is busiest.扩容是 O(n) 且要搬所有元素，所以装满的表在下一次 put 时，正好在最忙的时候付一大笔搬迁成本。
