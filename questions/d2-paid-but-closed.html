Follow-up 03追问 03

Money landed on an order that timed out 30 seconds ago.钱到账了，可这单 30 秒前刚超时关掉。

Do not swallow it, do not silently reopen it. Compare the timestamps, then refund or revive by rule.既不能吞掉，也不能偷偷重开。比时间戳，按规则退款或者复活。

Short answer先给答案CAS returns 0 rows and the status is CLOSED, so this is “paid but cancelled”. Decide by rule: if the close time is later than the pay time, refund automatically and mark REFUNDING; never leave the money stuck.CAS 影响 0 行、状态是 CLOSED → 这就是“已付但已取消”。按规则处理：关单时间晚于支付时间就自动退款、置 REFUNDING；绝不能让钱卡在原地。

must know必背3 min3 分钟

01Why it happens at all为什么会撞上

  • The user paid at 29:58; the timeout job closed the order at 30:01; the callback arrived at 30:02.用户 29:58 付款，超时任务 30:01 关单，回调 30:02 才到。
  • Payment succeeded on the platform side even though your order is closed — the two systems decided independently.支付平台那边成功了，你的订单这边已关闭——两个系统各自独立判断。
  • Clock skew between the pay server and your DB makes “who was first” a real question.支付服务器和你数据库的时钟差异，让“谁先”变成一个真问题。

02The decision table处理决策表

Condition条件Action动作
close_time < pay_time关单时间 < 支付时间the order was closed before the money arrived → refund先关单后付款 → 退款
close_time > pay_time (within grace)关单略晚于付款（容忍窗口内）revive the order, ship as normal复活订单，正常发货
product is out of stock by now此时货已卖完refund + notify, offer a replacement退款 + 通知，给替代方案

The grace window exists because closing an order is not instantaneous: you need a few seconds of tolerance for callbacks that were already in flight.容忍窗口的意义：关单不是瞬时的，对“已经在路上的回调”要留几秒余地。

03Never “fix” it by force别用强制手段“修”

  • Do not run update orders set status='PAID' without a condition — you lose the history and any concurrent job can resurrect it again.别不加条件地 update ... set status='PAID'——历史没了，别的任务再把它改回去。
  • Do not return success and do nothing. The platform thinks it is handled; the customer waits forever.别回成功却不做事。平台以为处理完了，客户永远等不到货。
  • Refund must itself be idempotent: one out_refund_no per order, same unique-index trick.退款本身也要幂等：一单一个 out_refund_no，还是唯一索引那一套。
